--what we are making--

1. JD (Job Description) Generator
Input: Hiring requirements (job title, skills, experience level)
Output: AI-generated, tailored job descriptions
2. ResumeRanker
Input: Job description & list of candidate resumes
Output: Ranked list of best-matching resumes
3. Email Automation
Function: Automates personalized email communication
Requirements: Simulated API calls for sending emails to candidates & hiring teams (e.g., interview invites)
4. InterviewScheduler
Function: Schedules interviews & sends calendar invites
Requirements: Google Calendar integration (or simulated API)
